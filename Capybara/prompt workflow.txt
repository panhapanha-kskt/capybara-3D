## Animation states I want (only these 7)
1. **Greeting (Khmer):** the capybara opens its eyes and does the Cambodian **sampeah** greeting: palms pressed together at chest level with a slight bow of the head and body, then a happy return to neutral. This goes with the greeting "សួស្តី" (Suostei). Do NOT render the Khmer text in Blender, because Blender's text objects don't shape Khmer script correctly. The website will show the text in a speech bubble. Plays once.
2. **Listening:** head tilts slightly toward the user, ears perk up, eyes open and attentive, gentle breathing. Loops.
3. **Thinking:** eyes look up or to the side, one paw taps or rests on the chin, a slow head sway. Optionally three small "..." dots bobbing above the head. Loops.
4. **Talking (lip-sync loop):** a generic talking loop that cycles mouth shapes (closed → A → O → E → closed) at a natural, irregular rhythm, with a small head bob and blinks. It must loop seamlessly so the website can play it for any length of reply. Also expose a simple **"mouth open" shape key**, so audio amplitude can drive it in the browser later if needed.
5. **Confused / error:** head tilts, one ear droops, a small "?" pops up above the head, then the capybara gives a small shrug. Plays once, then settles.
6. **Wake up:** starts from the current sleeping pose (closed eyes, head slightly down). It startles, blinks a few times, does a little shake, then ends in the idle/listening pose. Plays once.
7. **Goodbye:** a paw wave plus a slight bow, then the eyes close happily (^ ^). Plays once.
